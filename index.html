I'm Nayul (이나현), an industrial designer and brand creative based in Seoul. I specialize in turning ideas into sensory experiences — through packaging, identity systems, and visual campaigns that people feel before they fully understand.

Currently exploring global opportunities in creative agencies and studios across Germany, Australia, Japan, and the US. My work lives at the intersection of emotional intelligence and strategic design thinking.

I design like I think — methodically, but always led by instinct.
