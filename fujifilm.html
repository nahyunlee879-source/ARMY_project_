"Catch the moment before it's gone — the K-pop concert campaign for FUJIFILM."

K-pop concerts are among the most visually dense experiences on the planet. Thousands of phone screens light up simultaneously, fans chant in unison, and a single moment can define a relationship between an artist and their audience. Yet most of those moments are never truly captured — they're compressed into blurry phone videos, lost in the scroll.

This concept campaign positions FUJIFILM's Instax line as the antidote. The campaign line: "Don't watch it. Hold it." Every touchpoint — from arena signage to social content — reinforces the idea that a physical photograph is the only format that matches the emotional weight of the experience.

All key visuals were produced using Midjourney with custom-developed prompts and Runway ML for motion sequences. Post-production compositing handled in Photoshop.

Process

01

Campaign Strategy

Defined target audience (Gen Z K-pop fans aged 16–26), key insight (the paradox of watching a concert through a screen), and campaign platform: "real photographs, real presence."

02

AI Direction

Developed a visual language using Midjourney: film grain textures, concert light scatter, motion blur at specific shutter speeds. Iterated 200+ prompt variations to find the right balance of documentary and aesthetic.

03

Copywriting

Wrote campaign headlines, social copy, and OOH signage copy in both Korean and English. Tone: urgent, intimate, slightly poetic. "Don't watch it. Hold it." tested as the strongest line across 6 options.

04

Motion Concepts

Developed AI video prompt sequences using Runway ML, simulating a 30-second broadcast spot. Storyboarded 3 executions: arena entry, mid-show climax, and encore moment.
