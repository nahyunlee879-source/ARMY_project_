"Mir & Co. — a universe of characters, one cohesive world."

MIRCO is the comprehensive brand identity system for Mir & Co., a character-driven lifestyle brand built around a mascot ecosystem called Bubble Planet. Each product category corresponds to a distinct planet — and each planet has its own character, colour, and personality.

The design challenge was creating a system flexible enough to accommodate unlimited future characters, while maintaining visual cohesion. The solution: a modular logo architecture, a strict planetary colour taxonomy, and a set of construction rules that govern how characters can evolve across categories.

Process

01

Research

Competitor audit of character-led lifestyle brands. Referenced Sanrio, Line Friends, and Kakao Friends — identifying where current players fall short in system scalability.

02

Architecture

Designed the Bubble Planet taxonomy: each planet represents a product category. Defined visual rules — shape, colour temperature, expression vocabulary — for each planetary tier.

03

Characters

Developed 6 mascots across 3 planetary categories. Each mascot underwent 8–10 rounds of refinement balancing cuteness, distinctiveness, and cross-application versatility.

04

System

Compiled brand guidelines, packaging templates, digital asset library, and a usage handbook covering logo clear space, colour ratios, and character pairing rules.
