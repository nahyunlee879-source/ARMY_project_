"What if packaging could teach children to eat slowly?"

SLOWEAT is an children's snack brand built around a single behavioural insight: eating too fast is a learned habit, not a fixed one. The packaging is the product. Through deliberate structural design, every SLOWEAT box requires a small ritual to open — slowing the child down before the first bite.

The identity system bridges warmth and simplicity: an earthy olive palette, tactile paper finishes, and rounded type that feels approachable without being childish. The structural packaging mechanic — a folded paper drawer that unfolds one compartment at a time — physically embeds the brand's philosophy into the user experience.

Designed as a complete brand system: packaging architecture, logo, colour palette, pattern language, and point-of-sale materials.

Process

01

Research

Childhood eating psychology, slow-food movement references, and existing snack packaging audit. Identified the gap between fun packaging and mindful eating.

02

Concept

Explored three directions: timer-based portioning, story-based packaging, and structural friction. The friction mechanic was chosen for its tactile engagement and low-tech scalability.

03

Development

Paper engineering prototypes — 12 iterations of the drawer fold mechanism. Parallel development of the visual identity, typeface selection, and illustrated pattern system.

04

Final

Complete packaging system across 3 SKUs, brand guidelines, retail shelf mockup, and pattern application across secondary packaging and stickers.
