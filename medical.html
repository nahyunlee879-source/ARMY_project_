"Product photography without a prototype — using AI image generation to visualise an unrealised device."

This project explored the frontier of AI-assisted industrial product visualisation. The brief: create photorealistic product mockups for a concept medical beauty device — a handheld skin therapy tool — without any physical prototype, CAD model, or traditional rendering pipeline.

Using Nanobana, a specialised AI image generation tool, the work centred on developing a systematic prompt engineering methodology: controlling surface material (matte silicone, chrome accents), integrating logotype and surface typography directly into the generation process, and orchestrating consistent lighting across multiple product angles.

The result is a set of hero product shots indistinguishable from studio photography — achieved in a fraction of the time and cost of traditional product visualisation, while maintaining full creative control over brand language.

Process

01

Prompt Architecture

Developed a modular prompt system with locked and variable segments — locked elements controlled material and lighting consistency; variable elements changed angle, environment, and composition.

02

Form Development

Iterated 80+ generations to refine the device's ergonomic form: handle radius, button placement, charging port position. Each iteration evaluated against industrial design principles and aesthetic coherence.

03

Text Integration

Explored methods for embedding surface typography (brand name, regulatory markings) within AI-generated outputs — a technically complex challenge that typically requires post-production compositing. Developed a hybrid AI + Photoshop workflow.

04

Final Renders

Produced 12 final hero shots across 4 colorways and 3 viewing angles, plus a lifestyle shot series showing in-use scenarios. All images passed a blind evaluation against reference studio photography.
