Year

"Every one-room apartment is a dream in progress."

Young Koreans in their 20s are increasingly living alone in small studio apartments — 원룸, "one-rooms." These spaces are simultaneously the most constrained and the most personal places they inhabit. A campaign built for this generation shouldn't sell furniture; it should sell transformation.

Room for Dreams repositions IKEA as a partner in that transformation. The campaign follows three real-feeling characters as they reshape their 원룸 into a reflection of who they want to become: the late-night designer with a mood board wall, the budding home cook who turned a corner into a kitchen stage, the reader who turned a window alcove into their whole world.

Visuals were generated with Midjourney and animated with Runway ML, capturing the tactile warmth of lived-in spaces while maintaining IKEA's characteristic accessibility.

Campaign Mood Board

Process

01

Insight

Interviewed five people living in Seoul studio apartments about their relationship with their space. Identified a recurring theme: the apartment feels "in progress" — always one purchase away from being "real."

02

Platform

Defined campaign platform: "Your room is already a dream — IKEA helps you see it." Three character stories built around specific aspirations, not furniture categories.

03

Visual Direction

Developed AI visual prompts emphasising golden-hour window light, textured linen, and imperfect arrangements — the beauty of a space that is clearly lived in. Ran 150+ Midjourney iterations.

04

Motion & Story

Storyboarded three 60-second film concepts for OTT and social. Each film begins in a grey, empty room and ends in the same space, transformed — using the same camera position to maximise the contrast.
