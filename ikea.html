Campaign

IKEA Room
for Dreams

2024 Concept Campaign
Role
Campaign Strategy
Art Direction
AI Visual Production
Tools
Midjourney
Runway ML
Adobe Photoshop
Year
2024

"Every one-room apartment is a dream in progress."

Young Koreans in their 20s are increasingly living alone in small studio apartments — 원룸, "one-rooms." These spaces are simultaneously the most constrained and the most personal places they inhabit. A campaign built for this generation shouldn't sell furniture; it should sell transformation.

Room for Dreams repositions IKEA as a partner in that transformation. Three real-feeling characters reshape their 원룸 into a reflection of who they want to become. Visuals were generated with Midjourney and animated with Runway ML, capturing the tactile warmth of lived-in spaces.

Process

01

Insight

Interviewed five people living in Seoul studio apartments. Identified a recurring theme: the apartment feels "in progress" — always one purchase away from being "real."

02

Platform

"Your room is already a dream — IKEA helps you see it." Three character stories built around specific aspirations, not furniture categories.

03

Visual Direction

Developed AI visual prompts emphasising golden-hour window light, textured linen, and imperfect arrangements. Ran 150+ Midjourney iterations.

04

Motion & Story

Storyboarded three 60-second film concepts. Each film begins in a grey, empty room and ends in the same space, transformed — using the same camera position.
