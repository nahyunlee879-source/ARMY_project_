DreamDepot

2024 Brand Identity Project
Role

Logotype Design
Packaging
Tools

Photoshop
InDesign
Year
2024

"Stationery that carries dreams, not just ink."

DreamDepot is a stationery brand identity built on the idea that the tools you use to write and create should feel aspirational — not just functional. The brand targets young adults who treat their planners, notebooks, and pens as personal statements.

The logotype was custom-drawn: a wordmark balancing structured geometry with organic curves, evoking both precision and imagination. The colour palette — warm gold, cream, and soft terracotta — was chosen to feel at home on a desk without sacrificing distinctiveness on a shelf.

Process

01

Mood Research

Gathered reference imagery spanning European stationery brands, Korean indie paper goods, and lifestyle photography. Established emotional tone: warm, considered, quietly ambitious.

02

Logotype

Explored 14 logotype directions. Final direction drew from Bodoni proportions with custom terminal modifications to soften the geometric rigidity.

03

Colour & Materials

Material exploration with kraft paper, translucent vellum overlays, and foil stamp samples. Developed a two-tier packaging system: standard and gifting editions.

04

Application

Applied identity across notebooks, pen packaging, canvas pouches, and brand collateral. Documented full brand guidelines including font pairings and tone of voice.
